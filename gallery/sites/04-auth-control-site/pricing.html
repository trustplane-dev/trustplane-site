FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is TrustPlane Auth free?

Yes. The TrustPlane Auth runtime is free to start and runs in your own infrastructure. You can verify proof-bound requests in front of your APIs without a paid plan.

What do paid plans add?

Paid plans add TrustPlane Control: managed enrollment, signed policy distribution across your fleet, instant revocation, evidence-grade audit, multitenant management and RBAC. Enterprise adds self-hosting, SSO, SLAs and support.

Can I self-host TrustPlane Control?

Yes. The Enterprise plan supports running the full control plane inside your own environment for sovereignty and compliance alignment. Your data plane — the Auth deployments — always stays in your infrastructure.

Do I have to use Kubernetes?

No. The Auth runtime is Kubernetes-compatible but never Kubernetes-required. It runs at the edge, in clusters, on VMs, or as an agent sidecar.
