faq

Buying questions.

What exactly is an Auth Site?

One governed enforcement location — a verified distribution target for signed policy and trust material. “The boundary in front of the payments API in prod” is a site. Run Auth anywhere free; sites are the ones Control governs.

How do trust anchor source slots work?

Every included or purchased Auth Site carries its first automated source slot. Additional automated sources at a site take $29/mo add-on capacity allocated to that exact site. Manual vault onboarding and key rotation never consume capacity.

Why is the runtime free?

Enforcement in your infrastructure should never have a lock on it. The operational product — enrollment, releases, revocation, audit, roles — is what Enterprise pays for.

Can we evaluate without a sales call?

Yes. Develop is self-serve, free, unlimited in time. Sales exists for Enterprise rollout planning, Edge, and procurement — hello@trustplane.dev.

Annual terms? Volume?

Monthly by default; annual terms, volume, and design-partner arrangements by conversation. We're comfortable with security questionnaires and legal review.

Scope your rollout with an engineer.

Describe your agents, services, environments, and audit requirements. You'll get an architecture opinion, not a discovery-call script.
