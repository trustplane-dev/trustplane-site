FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is TrustPlane Auth really free?

Yes, genuinely. TrustPlane Auth is open source under the MIT licence. The auth plane runs in your own infrastructure, there are no usage limits, and you can put it in front of your APIs without talking to us first. We'd rather you read the code than fill in a form.

What does open source cover?

The auth plane itself: the verifier, the local broker, the reverse-proxy provider adapter, the CLI, the Helm chart, the Go and TypeScript SDKs, the conformance vectors, and the example integrations.

Is there a managed or paid offering?

Not today. Managed fleet governance — signed bundle distribution, revocation and audit at scale across many deployments — is a separate future direction and is not part of the open-source project. Nothing on this site is for sale.

Do I have to replace OAuth, IAM or my service mesh?

No, and please don't. The auth plane sits at the request boundary and consumes the identity you already have (SPIFFE, OIDC). It authorizes machine traffic per request. Your application still owns tenant, role, entitlement and resource decisions.

Do I need Kubernetes?

No. The provider adapter is happy in a cluster, but it also runs at the edge, on a VM, or as a sidecar next to an agent. Kubernetes-compatible, never Kubernetes-required.
