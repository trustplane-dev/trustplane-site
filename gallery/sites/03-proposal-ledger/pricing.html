Covenants of sale

The fine print, promoted to headline.

Clause 1
Your enforcement is not collateral.

On lapse or cancellation, existing Auth Sites, anchors, clients, and policy continue on the last signed state. Billing may block new activations; it may never revoke running access.

Clause 2
No meter on your own hardware.

Authorization decisions execute in your infrastructure. A flat base and two add-ons is the entire commercial catalog; there is no per-request line item and there never will be a surprising one.

Clause 3
Grandfathering is the default.

Should packaging change beneath an existing customer, existing capacity is grandfathered and reconciliation proceeds on the customer's schedule.

Clause 4
Renewal negotiations cannot become outages.

Edge licenses verify offline and carry explicit grace windows. The worst case of a commercial disagreement is a conversation, not downtime.

Questions of record

Matters of purchase.

What, precisely, is an Auth Site?

One governed enforcement location: a verified distribution target that receives signed policy and trust material from Control — “the boundary before the payments API, in production,” for instance. Auth may run anywhere at no charge; Auth Sites are the locations Control governs on your behalf.

Why is the runtime free?

Because enforcement inside your infrastructure should carry no lock. The commercial product is the operation of governance — enrollment, releases, revocation, audit, roles — which is precisely what an enterprise pays to have administered.

May we begin on Develop and adopt Enterprise later?

That is the intended sequence. Auth deployments already in service become governed Auth Sites upon connection to Control; nothing is reinstalled.

How does Edge licensing behave offline?

Edge licenses are Ed25519-signed artifacts verified locally, including in fully air-gapped estates. Validity windows and grace policy are explicit fields of the artifact, readable before signature.

Are annual terms or volume arrangements available?

Monthly is the default; annual terms, volume, and design-partner arrangements are matters for correspondence: hello@trustplane.dev. Security questionnaires and legal review are welcome, not endured.
