FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is TrustPlane Auth really free?

Yes, genuinely. The Auth runtime runs in your own infrastructure and you can put it in front of your APIs without talking to us first. We'd rather you try it than fill out a form.

What do the paid plans actually add?

Control is the part that gets painful by hand once you're past a couple of services. Managed enrollment, signing and shipping policy to your whole fleet, revocation that lands in seconds, and audit you can actually search. Enterprise adds self-hosting, SSO, and the support and paperwork bigger teams need.

Can I self-host Control?

Yes. On Enterprise you run the whole control plane in your own environment. Either way your data plane never leaves, because requests are always verified locally by Auth, in your infrastructure.

Do I have to rip out OAuth or my service mesh?

No, and please don't. TrustPlane sits at the request boundary and consumes the identity you already have (SPIFFE, OIDC). It's an authorization layer for machine traffic, not a replacement for your mesh or your identity provider.

Do I need Kubernetes?

No. The adapter is happy in a cluster, but it also runs at the edge, on a VM, or as a sidecar next to an agent. Kubernetes-compatible, never Kubernetes-required.
