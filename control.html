TrustPlane Control · the control plane

Decide who is trusted, once, for the whole fleet.

The auth plane answers one question per request. The control plane answers the bigger one: who is trusted, and what may they do. You define that here, sign it, ship it to every auth plane deployment, pull it back when something changes, and keep the record of what happened.

The trust lifecycle

From enrollment to enforcement, in one place.

01

Enroll

Callers enroll public keys against a trust anchor. Approval can be automatic for matching identities, or held for review.

02

Compile & sign

Your policy is compiled into a signed bundle that each auth plane deployment verifies cryptographically before applying.

03

Distribute

The signed bundle propagates to every auth plane deployment, with freshness tracking so stale policy never silently applies.

04

Revoke & audit

Revoke a key, client or anchor and watch it land across the fleet. Every decision is kept as an audit record.

Capabilities

What the control plane manages.

Trust anchors & enrollment

Define the identities you trust and how new callers enroll their public keys, with auto-approval rules for matching subjects.

Signed policy distribution

Compile, sign and ship policy bundles to the fleet, with version history and freshness guarantees.

Revocation

Revoke a client, an enrolled key, or a whole trust anchor, and propagate the change to every auth plane deployment.

Clients, keys & sessions

A client record is the stable anchor. Enrolled keys and sessions rotate and expire beneath it without changing who the client is.

Multitenant & fleets

Isolate tenants and environments; manage many auth plane deployments and sites from one control plane.

Evidence-grade audit

Search and export per-decision audit records and control-plane actions: who approved, revoked and published what.

Deployment

Your data plane never leaves.

The control plane distributes policy and collects evidence. It is never in the path of a request, which is what lets you run it wherever suits you without moving your traffic.

Run it yourself

The control plane runs in your own environment, alongside the auth plane deployments it governs.

Not in the request path

Requests are verified by the auth plane at your provider-owned boundary. A control plane outage does not stop verification.

Managed governance is a future direction

Running the signing and distribution plane as a managed service is a separate future direction, not part of the open-source project today.

FAQ

Auth plane, control plane.

What does the control plane do that the auth plane does not?

The auth plane decides individual requests, in the request path. The control plane decides who is trusted at all: it manages trust anchors and enrollment, compiles and signs policy, distributes it to every auth plane deployment, propagates revocation, and holds the audit records.

Can I run the auth plane without the control plane?

Yes. The auth plane verifies requests using local trust anchors and a signed policy bundle, so a single deployment works on its own, with no control plane and no external dependency per request. The control plane earns its place once you have more deployments than you want to configure by hand.

Where does the data plane run?

Always in your own infrastructure. Wherever the control plane runs, requests are verified by the auth plane at your provider-owned boundary. Request bodies do not leave your environment to be authorized.

Is managed fleet governance available?

Not yet. Running the signing and distribution plane as a managed service is a separate future direction and is not part of the open-source project today.
