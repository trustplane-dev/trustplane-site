FAQ

Subscription questions.

Why call this subscriptions instead of pricing?

Machine authorization usually becomes an operating capability, not a one-time tool. The package starts with Auth and scales into Control governance, support, and deployment choices.

Can we evaluate without a sales call?

Yes. Start from Downloads and Docs. Sales is mainly for Control, enterprise deployment planning, and procurement.

Does managed Control see our API traffic?

The intended architecture keeps TrustPlane Auth enforcement in your infrastructure. Control governs policy, enrollment, revocation, and audit operations rather than acting as a per-request data-plane hop.

Who should join the buying conversation?

Security engineering, platform engineering, infrastructure, and the product team responsible for agent or machine workflows. For Enterprise, include compliance and procurement early.
