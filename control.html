TrustPlane Control · the governance plane

Govern trust across your entire fleet.

TrustPlane Control is where you decide who's trusted and what they can do, then sign that policy and ship it to every Auth deployment, pull it back in seconds, and prove what happened. It's multitenant and fleet-aware. Run it as a managed service, or host the whole thing yourself.

The trust lifecycle

From enrollment to enforcement, in one place.

01

Enroll

Callers enroll through trust anchors. Approvals can be automatic for matching identities, or held for review.

02

Compile & sign

Control compiles your policy into a signed bundle the Auth runtime can verify cryptographically.

03

Distribute

Signed policy propagates to every Auth deployment in the fleet, with freshness tracking.

04

Revoke & audit

Revoke a key or anchor and watch it land across the fleet. Every decision is recorded as evidence.

Capabilities

Enterprise governance, built in.

Trust anchors & enrollment

Define the identities you trust and how new callers enroll, with auto-approval policies for matching subjects.

Signed policy distribution

Compile, sign and ship policy bundles across the fleet, with version history and freshness guarantees.

Instant revocation

Revoke a client, key, or anchor and propagate the change to every Auth deployment in seconds.

Multitenant & fleets

Isolate tenants and environments; manage many Auth fleets and sites from a single control plane.

Evidence-grade audit

Search and export per-request decisions and control-plane actions: who approved, revoked, and published what.

Roles & access (RBAC)

Platform and tenant roles, scoped permissions, and least-privilege controls for your team.

Deployment

Managed, or run it yourself.

Most teams start on the managed service and never look back. When your security or compliance posture needs it, the same control plane runs entirely inside your own environment.

Managed service (default)

We run Control; you manage policy. The data plane, your Auth deployments themselves, never leaves your infrastructure.

Self-hosted (enterprise)

Run the full control plane in your own environment for maximum sovereignty and compliance alignment.

Your data plane never leaves

Regardless of where Control runs, requests are verified locally by Auth, in your infrastructure.
