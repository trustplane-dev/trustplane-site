Governance model

The control plane for authorization operations.

Static credentials turn machine access into a thousand local decisions. Control centralizes the operational work without pulling your data plane out of your environment.

Enroll machine identities.Define trust anchors, accepted callers, approval rules, and environment boundaries for agent and service access.
Publish signed policy.Distribute policy to Auth deployments with versioning, freshness, and operational visibility.
Revoke and prove.Pull access quickly, track propagation, and keep audit evidence connected to runtime decisions.

FAQ

Control questions.

What does TrustPlane Control add?

Control adds trust anchor management, enrollment workflows, policy distribution, revocation, fleet visibility, audit search, roles, and enterprise deployment options.

Can teams start with Auth before buying Control?

Yes. The preview path is designed for teams to start with Auth and add Control when they need fleet governance, policy distribution, revocation, and audit workflows.

Is self-hosted Control available?

Self-hosted Control is positioned for enterprise teams with sovereignty, compliance, or internal platform requirements.

Does managed Control see our API traffic?

The intended architecture keeps Auth enforcement in your infrastructure. Control governs policy, enrollment, revocation, and audit operations rather than acting as a per-request data-plane hop.
